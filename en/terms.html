Terms & conditions

Last updated: January 2026

Appointments

Appointments can be made online, by phone or by email. For your first visit, please arrive 10 minutes early to complete your registration.

Cancelling or rescheduling

You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. For cancellations within 24 hours or no-shows, we reserve the right to charge (part of) the consultation fee.

Payment

Payment is due directly after treatment, by card or via a digital payment link. You'll receive an invoice that you can submit to your health insurer yourself.

Treatment relationship

Osteopathy complements, but does not replace, mainstream medical care. If in doubt, we will refer you to a GP or specialist.

Complaints

Not satisfied with a treatment or our service? Please contact us so we can find a solution together. Our osteopaths are affiliated with the professional association's complaints procedure.

Governing law

These terms are governed by Dutch law.
