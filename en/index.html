Our approach

Osteopathy looks beyond the complaint alone

Using gentle, manual techniques we examine and treat tension and restricted movement in muscles, joints, organs and connective tissue — supporting your body's own ability to heal.

  • A thorough intake and examination of your whole body
  • Gentle, manual techniques — no forceful cracking
  • Practical advice on posture, movement and recovery
