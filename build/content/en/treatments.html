Treatments

For every body, at every age

Osteopathy can help with a wide range of complaints — from acute pain to recurring tension. Below you'll find what we can help you with.

Back, neck & joint complaints

The most common reason to visit an osteopath. We examine how your whole body moves and treat not only the site of the pain, but also underlying causes.

  • Lower back pain & acute back pain
  • Neck & shoulder complaints
  • Herniated disc complaints
  • Whiplash
  • Arthritis & joint stiffness
  • RSI & overuse

Headaches, jaw & dizziness

Tension in the neck, jaw or head can lead to recurring headaches or a light-headed feeling. Releasing this tension often brings noticeable relief.

  • Tension headaches
  • Migraine complaints
  • Jaw complaints (TMD)
  • Dizziness
  • Teeth grinding
  • Tinnitus (related)

Babies & children

Birth is a major physical event — for the baby too. With very gentle techniques, suited to a small body, we support a good start.

  • Preferential posture
  • Excessive crying / colic
  • Feeding difficulties
  • Disrupted sleep
  • Growing pains
  • Posture & motor development

Pregnancy & postpartum recovery

During and after pregnancy your body changes significantly. Osteopathy can help relieve complaints and support recovery.

  • Pelvic & pelvic floor complaints
  • Lower back pain during pregnancy
  • Recovery after a caesarean section
  • Postural complaints
  • Fatigue
  • Preparation for birth

Sports & injury prevention

Athletes often place uneven demands on their bodies. We help with recovery after an injury, but also look at movement patterns to prevent recurrence.

  • Muscle & tendon injuries
  • Ankle & knee complaints
  • Overuse complaints
  • Recovery after injury
  • Movement analysis
  • Preventive maintenance

Stress, fatigue & breathing

Long-term stress often shows up physically: feeling on edge, shallow breathing or digestive complaints. Osteopathy can help your body find rest again.

  • Tension complaints
  • Fatigue
  • Breathing complaints
  • Digestive complaints
  • Sleep problems
  • Overall wellbeing
