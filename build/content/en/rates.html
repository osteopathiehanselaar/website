Reimbursement

Covered by your supplementary insurance

Osteopathy is not part of the basic Dutch health insurance, but is reimbursed by almost all insurers through a supplementary package. The amount of reimbursement varies per insurer and package.

We are affiliated with the professional association, which is required for reimbursement by virtually all insurers. After your treatment you'll receive an invoice you can easily submit yourself.

Good to know

Cancelling or rescheduling

Can't make it to your appointment? Let us know at least 24 hours in advance and we won't charge you. For later cancellations or no-shows, we may need to charge (part of) the consultation fee. Read more in our terms & conditions.
