About us

A calm practice in the heart of Bemelen

Osteopathie Hanselaar was founded on the belief that good care starts with truly listening — to your story and to your body.

Our story

From complaint to cause

After years working in mainstream healthcare, I made a deliberate choice for osteopathy: a discipline that takes the time to truly understand where a complaint comes from.

I help clients of all ages — from babies to seniors — with a personal approach in a calm, light-filled practice in the heart of Bemelen.

I work closely with GPs, physiotherapists and midwives in the region, so you always get the care that suits you best.

Levi Hanselaar

Osteopath MSc. DO-MRO

Guides clients of all ages with a personal, calm approach — from back and joint complaints to babies and sports.

Ever since I was a child, I've dreamed of understanding the human body as well as I possibly could. That curiosity is the common thread running through my training and the way I work: calm, attentive, and always looking for the cause behind a complaint. Outside the practice you'll often find me on the sports field, which only strengthens my connection with athletes and their injuries.

Education

  • Physiotherapy, Zuyd University of Applied Sciences, Heerlen (2019-2023)
  • Osteopathy, International Academy of Osteopathy, Antwerp (2023-2027)

Special interests

Orofacial complaints, digestive complaints and athletes.

Bente Hompesch

Osteopath MSc. DO-MRO

With her background in physiotherapy and osteopathy, Bente offers warm, expert care — with special attention for pregnancy, the pelvis and the youngest patients.

Bente combines a broad foundation in physiotherapy with a follow-up training in osteopathy, and so always looks at the body as a whole. With her patient, attentive approach, (expecting) parents and young children quickly feel at ease. She likes to work closely with midwives and paediatricians in the region, so you or your little one get exactly the right care.

Education

  • Physiotherapy, Zuyd University of Applied Sciences, Heerlen (2019-2023)
  • Osteopathy, International Academy of Osteopathy, Antwerp (2024-2028)

Special interests

Pelvic issues, pregnancy, babies & children and neurological conditions.
