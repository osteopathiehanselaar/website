Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: January 2026

What information do we collect?

When you book an appointment or fill in a form, we collect your name, contact details and information about your complaint. During treatment we record relevant medical information in a patient file.

Why do we use this information?

We use your information to schedule appointments, provide treatment, communicate with you, and to comply with our legal (financial and medical) record-keeping obligations.

How long do we keep your information?

Medical records are kept in line with the legal retention period of 20 years. Contact details from the request form are kept no longer than necessary to handle your enquiry.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties, except where necessary for your treatment (for example with your GP, with your consent) or where legally required.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or (in certain cases) have your information deleted. Contact us via levi@fysiobemelen.nl.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. Any technical cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Get in touch via our contact page.
